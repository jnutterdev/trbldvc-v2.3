This album features a collaboration between three independent dark ambient arists. The music itself is exactly what you’d picture a gritty crime noir soundtrack to sound like. Complete with smokey atmospheres, subtle jazz percussion and horns, and the occasional tape loops. Highly recommended for anyone into dark ambient and film noir.
