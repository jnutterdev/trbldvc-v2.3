The latest release on Abstrakt Relfections brings us 9 epic tracks by Microscopists. The album begins with the sort of atmospheric clicks and cuts as one would expect from other releases, but later songs like “Morbid Calculus” ties in an intricate weave of post-rock style guitars and some minimal voices; think something off of early to mid Neurot Recordings but with the vocal style of, say, Katatonia. Needless to say, this is refreshing release and definitely worth the donation. Check it out.
