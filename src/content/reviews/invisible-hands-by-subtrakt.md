Just shy of thirty minutes, this ep picks up where last year’s Inside Information left off. Three tracks of elegant, shimmering tones that seem to flow into each other very nicely. Compared to previous albums, these tracks have very minmal percussive elements that are accompanied by pianos but stil manages to find the right balance. The fourth tracks is an atmospheric remix of Across The Grain by Monolog which fits very nicely with the rest of the track to the point where you almost wouldn’t recoginize it being a remix.
