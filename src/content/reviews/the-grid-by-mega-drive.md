This is a fairly new release, which came out sometime toward the end of 2017. When I first bought it, I thought it was OK–not quite the same drive, production, and overall style as 198XAD; which is what I was hoping for based on the title. It’s definitely a step in the same direction that Seas of Infinity took, which I thought was OK as well. The more I listen to this album now, I’ve grown more attached to it as a whole. While it is a bit more on the ambient side, it continues to capture the nostalgia of 80s synthwave, which I appreciate more. I would definitely recommend this to anyone into synthwave, and will probably go back and listen to Seas of Inifity again.
